# **App Name**: Ramadhan Takjil Order

## Core Features:

- Menu Display: Display available Takjil items fetched directly from the connected Google Sheet.
- Order Placement: Allow users to select items and specify quantities for their order.
- Order Summary: Show a summary of the order with all items, quantities, prices and the total amount before the order is submitted.
- Delivery Fee Calculation: Automatically add a fixed delivery fee of Rp. 2.500 to each order.
- Order Submission: Submit the order and store the data, including user details, ordered items, and total amount, to the Google Sheet.
- Payment Method Selection: Enable users to select their preferred payment method, either 'QRIS' or 'COD'.
- AI-Powered Recommendation Tool: AI tool suggest items to users based on the items being most frequently ordered at that time of day.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A warm orange (#FF8C00), reminiscent of traditional lanterns, creating a welcoming feel.
- Background color: A light, desaturated beige (#F5F5DC) provides a soft and clean background.
- Accent color: A muted yellow (#D3D33D), drawing from images of dates, complements the orange, adding a touch of tradition and appetite appeal.
- Body and headline font: 'PT Sans', a humanist sans-serif that combines a modern look and a little warmth or personality.
- Use clean, simple icons to represent different food categories and payment methods.
- Employ a clear, grid-based layout for easy navigation and readability, especially on mobile devices.
- Incorporate subtle animations, such as a smooth transition when adding items to the cart or confirming an order.
